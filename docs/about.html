Unix & enterprise storage

I’m Rahul Luthra, a Senior Unix/Linux Systems Administrator with more than 20 years of experience building and supporting production infrastructure. I started with Sun servers and Solaris, then joined Oracle’s storage team, where I worked on highly available NetApp systems, storage migrations, and replication. That work taught me to trace problems across the server, network, and storage layers.

Production engineering

At JPMorgan Chase, I helped the Unix engineering team support production systems and deliver infrastructure builds. At the London Metal Exchange, I supported trading and clearing infrastructure and defined Nagios monitoring standards. At HSBC, I led infrastructure delivery across more than 15 programmes, including Red Hat Enterprise Linux builds, Veritas clusters with replicated storage, data centre migrations, and disaster recovery tests.

Automation & observability

At EquiLend, I’ve brought that production experience into automation and observability. I build monthly patching workflows with Ansible, AWX, and Git, integrating VMware vCenter and Red Hat Satellite. I also administer Kubernetes nodes and configure Prometheus and Grafana monitoring and alerts.

The portfolio

This portfolio shows how I approach the work. The Linux labs cover CPU contention, JVM memory, NFS, network performance, and service reliability. Each case study walks through the symptoms, the evidence I used to narrow down the cause, and how I checked whether the fix worked.
